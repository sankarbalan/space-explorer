        Built by people who like asking why.

        Space Explorer exists because the best way to understand a big number is to see it, move it and compare it. We combine published data with live simulations, and we label what is known, what is debated and what is guesswork.

        How we keep it honest

        1. Evidence levels. Every claim about life is placed on a five-step ladder, from speculation to confirmed. The top step is still empty.
        2. Sources named. Data comes from NASA, ESA and ISRO fact sheets and from papers we cite on each entry.
        3. Visuals are explainers. Where a simulation illustrates an idea rather than shows a photograph, we say so.
        4. Reviewed regularly. Content last reviewed recently. Moon counts and mission dates change; we update them.
